One keyboard.

More computers.

Share your keyboard and mouse across computers with a dedicated ESP32 controller and a USB receiver for each computer.

How it works ↓ All projects

A shared setup, a different destination

How it works

  1. 01 / Connect

    One place for your inputs.

    Connect your keyboard and mouse through a USB hub to the ESP32-S3 controller.

  2. 02 / Pair

    A receiver for each computer.

    Plug an ESP32-S2 USB receiver into each computer and pair it with the controller. Each receiver has its own number, from 1 to 7.

  3. 03 / Switch

    Choose where you work.

    Double-tap Ctrl, then press 1–7 to choose a paired computer. The trigger key is configurable.

This is just the beginning.

KMSwitcher is in active development, and this page is its new home. Hardware details, setup guides, and downloads will follow as the site grows.
